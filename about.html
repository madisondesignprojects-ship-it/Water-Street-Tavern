About Water Street Tavern

Mission Statement

An Experience Worth Returning To

Water Street Tavern (WST) is operated by a talented team with an extensive and unique skillset that will allow us to fulfill our goal to be one of the best dining establishments in the area. Our principles are to provide a safe, clean, relaxed environment for everyone; and to be a quality serving establishment for the community.

We serve a mix of exceptional burgers, classic comfort food, and elevated entrees in our beautiful, riverfront space. We approach customer service with appreciation and believe it is a privilege to serve our guests.

Our commitment goes beyond great food. We strive to be a safe place where everyone, staff included, feel welcome and respected. We celebrate open-mindedness, diversity, and inclusiveness.

“The WST family welcomes you. Whether you're joining us for a casual meal, a special celebration, or simply to unwind by the water; come as you are.”

Our goal is simple: to give you an experience worth returning to, again and again.
