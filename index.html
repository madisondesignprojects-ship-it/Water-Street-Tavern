News & Updates

This Season

Fall is in the air, but the patio is still here for you!

We're getting a chill in the air, so we've enclosed the covered patio. It now provides a cozy, heated, four-season dining area that retains its great views of the river, with the same full service as the main dining room.

The outdoor fire pits are still here for you, and a great space for the hearty to hang out and enjoy a cocktail or a hot beverage!
