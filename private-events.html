The Setting

Our Spaces

The best fit for your gathering depends on your date and guest count. Tell us about your event and we'll help you plan it.

Inside

The Dining Room

Our beautiful, riverfront space with a retro supper club feel, where we serve a mix of exceptional burgers, classic comfort food and elevated entrées.

Four-Season

The Covered Patio

Enclosed for the cooler months, the covered patio is a cozy, heated, four-season dining area that keeps its great views of the river, with the same full service as the main dining room. The outdoor fire pits are a great spot for a cocktail or a hot beverage.

Food & Drink

From Our Kitchen & Bar

Supper club classics, steaks, Friday fish fry, Wisconsin-style old fashioneds and a thoughtful wine list. Ask us about food and drink options for your event.
